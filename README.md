# controls-and-compliance-checklist
Internal IT Audit and Risk Assessment – Botium Toys Case Study

## Project Overvue
This project presents an internal IT audit conducted for a fictional organization, Botium Toys, to assess its security posture, identify potential risks, and evaluate compliance requirements.
The assessment applies the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) and considers relevant compliance requirements, including PCI DSS and GDPR.

## Scenario
This project is based on a simulated scenario created for educational and portfolio purposes.
Botium Toys is a fictional U.S.-based company experiencing rapid business growth and an expanding online presence. The organization requires an internal IT audit to identify security risks, vulnerabilities, and potential compliance gaps.

## Project Objective 
The objective of this audit is to evaluate the organization's current security posture, identify potential risks and vulnerabilities, and assess compliance requirements related to online payment processing and business operations involving the European Union.

## Scope
The audit scope includes the organization's managed IT assets, security controls, access management practices, and compliance requirements related to payment processing and EU data protection.

## Method
The audit was conducted using the NIST Cybersecurity Framework as a reference. The assessment included defining the audit scope and objectives, reviewing managed assets, identifying potential risks, evaluating existing controls, and documenting compliance gaps and recommendations.

1. Defined the audit scope and objectives.
2. Reviewed the organization's managed assets.
3. Identified potential risks, threats, and vulnerabilities.
4. Evaluated existing security controls.
5. Assessed relevant compliance requirements.
6. Documented findings and recommendations.

## Framework and Compliance References 
NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
PCI DSS
General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

These references were used to evaluate the organization's security controls and identify potential compliance gaps within the scope of the assessment.

## Recommendations
Based on the audit findings, Botium Toys should prioritize improvements in access control, credential management, threat detection, operational resilience, and data protection. Key actions include implementing least privilege, separation of duties, password policies, a password management system, an intrusion detection system, disaster recovery planning, legacy system management, encryption, and asset classification.
These measures would help reduce the risk of unauthorized access, improve the detection of security threats, strengthen operational resilience, and better protect sensitive information while supporting the organization's compliance obligations.
